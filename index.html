Job Scam Check Rules 2026.09.30.2

Is this job real?

Paste a job posting or a recruiter's message. It's checked against known job-scam patterns (fees to start, fake checks, money-mule work, gift-card pay, chat-app interviews and more), and every warning shows the exact words that triggered it.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere or saved.

A pattern match is a reason to slow down, not proof either way. A clean result can still be a scam, so always confirm the job on the employer's own website before sharing personal details. Report job scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov. Rulepack 2026.09.30.2 · 33 active rules.
